Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Schedule Text Reminders (shown as “Follow-Ups” on your phone, and called “the app” or “Follow-Ups” below) is a mobile app made by EDDEVELOPED ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information you put into the app. The short version: everything you enter (your customers, reminders, messages and templates) stays on your own device. We don't collect it, receive it, or have any way to see it. From version 1.2, the app also sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can improve it. They never include anything you typed, and you can turn them off in the app.

Information you enter

To do its job, the app stores what you type into it: customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, Telegram usernames and notes; your reminders and the messages attached to them; your message templates; and your settings, such as your business name, default reminder time, default send method and country code. This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device only.

What we collect: usage statistics and crash reports

Follow-Ups has no accounts and no servers of its own. Version 1.2 and later use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, services from Google, to send us anonymous information about how the app is used and when it crashes. (Earlier versions send nothing.) This information is:

  • How the app is used: which screens are opened and which features are used, for example that a reminder was created, which send method it uses (text, email, WhatsApp, Telegram or Google Voice), whether a template was used, whether Messages opened, and whether the reminder was marked completed or snoozed.
  • Rounded counts and settings: for example “6–20 customers” or “1–5 overdue reminders”, your default send method, default reminder time and country code, whether notifications are on, and whether a business name is set (never the name itself).
  • Crash and error reports: the type of error and where in the app's code it happened, plus the screen that was open.
  • Information the services collect automatically: a random identifier for this installation of the app, the app version, the device model, iOS version and language, when the app is opened and for how long, and an approximate location (country, region and city) that Google works out from the device's internet address.

Never included: customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, Telegram usernames or notes; message or template text; your business name; or anything else you type into the app. Follow-Ups never reads your phone's contacts.

Not used to track you or for advertising. Follow-Ups does not use your device's advertising identifier, does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites, has advertising features switched off, and does not link this information to your identity. We do not sell or share it. Google processes it for us under its own terms; see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How long it's kept: usage statistics for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

Turning it off

In the app, open Settings › Share usage data and switch it off. The app then stops sending usage statistics and crash reports, deletes any it hasn't sent yet, and starts with a new random identifier if you switch it back on. Because the information isn't linked to you, we can't look up or delete one person's records, but turning it off stops all further collection.

Sending messages

When you tap Open Messages, Open Email, Open WhatsApp, Open Telegram or Share, Follow-Ups hands the recipient and message to an app on your phone (for example Messages, Mail, WhatsApp or Telegram) so you can review and send it yourself. For Google Voice, Follow-Ups copies the message to your phone's clipboard and opens Google Voice so you can paste it. Follow-Ups never sends a message on its own. Once a message is in another app, that app and its provider (such as Apple, Google, WhatsApp, Telegram, your mobile carrier or your email provider) handle it under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the phone's own notification system. A reminder notification shows the customer's name but never the message text. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings; your reminders still appear inside the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you when a follow-up is due.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android only): so reminders arrive at the exact time you chose.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone, and it does not ask to track you.

Backups

If you use your phone's backup service (such as iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup), your device may include Follow-Ups' data in those backups. Apple or Google stores those backups under their own policies and your account settings.

Deleting your information

You can delete any customer, reminder or template inside the app. Deleting a customer also deletes their reminders. Uninstalling Follow-Ups removes all of its data from your device. Copies in your phone's backups are managed through your Apple or Google account. For usage statistics and crash reports, see “Turning it off” above.

Your customers' information

The contact details you enter belong to you and your customers. You are responsible for having permission to contact them and for following the laws that apply to the messages you send.

Children

Follow-Ups is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app collects any other information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
