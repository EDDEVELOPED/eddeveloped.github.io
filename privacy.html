Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Follow-Ups is a mobile app made by EDDEVELOPED ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information you put into the app. The short version: everything you enter stays on your own device. We don't collect it, receive it, or have any way to see it.

Information you enter

To do its job, the app stores what you type into it: customer names, phone numbers, email addresses and notes; your reminders and the messages attached to them; your message templates; and your settings, such as your business name and default reminder time. This information is saved in the app's private storage on your device only.

What we collect

Nothing. Follow-Ups has no accounts and no servers. It does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services, and it does not send your information anywhere over the internet. We do not sell or share data, because we never have it.

Sending messages

When you tap Open Messages, Open Email or Share, Follow-Ups hands the recipient and message to an app on your phone (for example Messages or Mail) so you can review and send it yourself. Follow-Ups never sends a message on its own. Once a message is in another app, that app and its provider (such as Apple, Google, your mobile carrier or your email provider) handle it under their own privacy policies.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device by the phone's own notification system. A reminder notification shows the customer's name but never the message text. You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone's settings; your reminders still appear inside the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you when a follow-up is due.
  • Alarms & reminders (Android only): so reminders arrive at the exact time you chose.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.

Backups

If you use your phone's backup service (such as iCloud Backup or Google's Android backup), your device may include Follow-Ups' data in those backups. Apple or Google stores those backups under their own policies and your account settings.

Deleting your information

You can delete any customer, reminder or template inside the app. Deleting a customer also deletes their reminders. Uninstalling Follow-Ups removes all of its data from your device. Copies in your phone's backups are managed through your Apple or Google account.

Your customers' information

The contact details you enter belong to you and your customers. You are responsible for having permission to contact them and for following the laws that apply to the messages you send.

Children

Follow-Ups is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children, or from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future version of the app ever collects information, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
