Prepare once. Get reminded. Send.

Follow-Up Reminders - SMS helps small businesses keep in touch with customers. Write a follow-up message ahead of time, pick the day, and the app reminds you when it's due. One tap opens your phone's Messages or Mail app with the customer's number and your message already filled in. You review it and press Send yourself.

  • Customers, reminders and message templates in one place
  • Reminders for today, overdue and coming up
  • Templates that fill in the customer's name, the date and your business name
  • Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking

Available for iPhone and Android. On your phone it appears as “Follow-Ups”.
