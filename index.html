Prepare once. Get reminded. Send.

Schedule Text Reminders helps small businesses keep in touch with customers. Write a follow-up message ahead of time, pick the day, and the app reminds you when it's due. One tap opens Messages, Mail, WhatsApp or Telegram with the customer and your message already filled in. For Google Voice, the app copies your message and opens Google Voice so you can paste it. You review it and press Send yourself.

  • Customers, reminders and message templates in one place
  • Reminders for today, overdue and coming up
  • Send by text, email, WhatsApp, Telegram or Google Voice
  • Templates that fill in the customer's name, the date, their appointment and your business name
  • Everything stays on your phone: no account, no ads, no tracking

Available for iPhone. On your phone it appears as “Follow-Ups”.
