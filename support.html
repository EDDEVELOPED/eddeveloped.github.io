Support

Need help with Follow-Ups? Email eddevteam@gmail.com and include your phone model. Please don't send customer details.

My reminder notifications don't arrive

  • Check that notifications are on for Follow-Ups in your phone's Settings. The app's Settings tab shows their status and can send a test notification.
  • Android: allow "Alarms & reminders" for Follow-Ups (the app offers this in Settings). Without it, Android may hold reminders until you unlock the phone.
  • iPhone: a Focus mode or Do Not Disturb can silence notifications. Allow Follow-Ups in that Focus.
  • Due and overdue follow-ups always appear on the Today screen, even if a notification was missed.

Messages or Mail opened without my text

Some messaging apps don't accept a pre-filled message. Use Copy or Share on the reminder screen, then paste it into your messaging app.

Was my message sent?

Follow-Ups can't see what happens in your Messages or Mail app. When you come back, it asks whether you sent the message. Mark the reminder completed only if you pressed Send.

Moving to a new phone

Your data is stored only on your phone. Restoring a full phone backup (iCloud or Google) to the new phone may bring it along. There is no online account to sign in to.

Deleting my data

Delete customers, reminders or templates in the app, or uninstall Follow-Ups to remove everything from the phone. See the privacy policy for details.
